How this is calculated

  • Only Arrowhead's published collection-rate lift changes rupees collected. It applies to your human collection rate in each bucket, capped at 100% of what is overdue.
  • Escalated calls collect at your own human rate, minus borrowers lost in the transfer. The transfer leg is billed by your telco at your rate.
  • P&L impact is credit cost avoided minus the change in collection cost. A collected EMI is cash; the P&L gain is the part of it that would otherwise have become provisions or write-offs.
  • The connection lift, the surge-day gap and 10-second hang-ups explain the result. They are never added to it.
  • Arrowhead against your current vendor is derived through your human collection rate in the same bucket: your vendor's rate is the before, and your human rate × Arrowhead's published lift is the after. If you no longer use human agents, the rate from before you moved to AI stands in, and the page says so.
  • The vendor comparison covers only the accounts your vendor works. Your human agents and seats are left as they are.
  • RBI directions effective 1 January 2027 limit recovery calls to 8 am to 7 pm unless the borrower asks for another time.
